Ziyang Pan

I am a researcher working on foundation models and agents. I am currently affiliated with z.ai (Zhipu AI), contributing to the GLM series of foundation models. I have been a member of GLM team since 2023, supervised by Prof. Jie Tang at Tsinghua University. Before that, I received my Bachelor's and Master's degrees at Sun Yat-sen University, under the supervision of Prof. Daifeng Li.

My research interests include foundation models, agents, and physical intelligence.
